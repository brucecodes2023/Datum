\subsection{BW-SIM-01 thermal --- Built, 30 Oct: steady and transient heat, one-way thermal stress, HDT bands}\label{sec:sim-thermal-built}
%======================================================================

Thermal and Thermal stress now run on the frame of \S\ref{sec:sim01-built}, built on your answers H1--H5
(\S\ref{sec:sim-thermal-plan}). Code: \file{src/Mod/BruceWorx/bruceworx\_sim\_thermal.py} (headless), the panels in
\file{bruceworx\_sim\_gui.py}; commits ``SIM-01: Thermal and Thermal stress studies\ldots'' and ``SIM-01: thermal panels,
Setup commands\ldots''.

\paragraph{What you can do now.}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{New Study} offers Thermal (a \emph{Transient} box with end time and step, initial temperature) and
        Thermal stress (the stress-free $T_{ref}$). Thermal is a CalculiX \code{*HEAT TRANSFER}; thermal stress is
        \code{*UNCOUPLED TEMPERATURE-DISPLACEMENT, STEADY STATE}, which solves the temperature field and then the stress it
        causes in one step (one way, as planned).
  \item \textbf{Setup} (ribbon and study panel): Temperature, Heat flux, Heat source (watts, spread over the body's volume;
        a body of several solids shares the power by volume) and Convection with presets still air 10, light airflow 25,
        fan or prop wash 75\,W/m$^2$K or a typed value, ambient temperature, and a note that these are estimates.
  \item \textbf{Pre-check}: $k$ (plus $\alpha$ for thermal stress, $\rho$ and $c_p$ for a transient run), at least one
        temperature or convection boundary, supports for thermal stress, times that make at most 2000 steps.
  \item \textbf{Results}: Temperature and HDT bands (each element in the band of its hottest node: red at or above the heat
        deflection temperature, amber within the margin, 10\,K by default, settable per study; the limit can also be typed
        over the card's). Transient: a result per time step (at most 60 kept), a time-step picker, the temperature of the
        hottest node and of a probe node against time, and the time to reach the limit. Thermal stress adds the static views
        and the safety factor.
  \item \textbf{Report}: setup, temperatures, the band table, the pgfplots curve, and the heat balance (heat in against heat out).
  \item \textbf{HDT on the cards}: every plastic and printed Keystone card has \code{HeatDeflectionTemperature}, basis and
        source in its \code{BruceWorx:} block (ISO 75 / ASTM D648 at the heavier load, 1.8\,MPa; the 0.45\,MPa value is in the
        basis). The ten printed grades are read from the Bambu data sheets (e.g.\ PLA 54, PETG 65, ABS 84, PA12-CF 170\,$^\circ$C),
        POM-C and UHMW-PE from their data sheets, four machined plastics (Delrin 150, PA6, PC sheet, PMMA) from handbook
        typicals marked ``check the data sheet''. TPU 95A has none (the sheet says N/A); metals and composites have none.
        All 33 cards regenerate from the extended \file{make\_cards.py} with only those lines added.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Decided while building.}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{One uncoupled step, not two.} ``A thermal step then a static step'' is what \code{*UNCOUPLED TEMPERATURE-DISPLACEMENT}
        does inside one step, with the same one-way result and no temperature hand-over file.
  \item \textbf{The held bar is 300\,mm.} On the plan's 100\,mm bar the clamped ends, which also hold the sideways growth, still
        reach the mid-length: $-130.3$\,MPa ($+3.4$\,\%). At 300\,mm it is $-127.4$. The free-growth case stays at 100\,mm.
  \item \textbf{CalculiX needs an initial temperature} for any thermomechanical step, so the study owns an Initial temperature
        object (all nodes) driven by the study's setting.
  \item \textbf{Heat balance} without a second solve: the heat at the fixed temperatures from CalculiX \code{*NODE PRINT, RFL}
        totals, convection and flux integrated over the faces named in the written input (exact for the quadratic mid-node
        rule), sources from their watts. Transient runs show the terms and the heat stored, not a closed balance.
  \item \textbf{Touching bodies} of a container are fused (\code{Part::MultiFuse}) for thermal studies, so they share one
        conforming mesh and are perfectly bonded; one material per study for now.
  \item \textbf{Fixed time steps} (\code{DIRECT}) for a transient run, so results fall on the step times; backward Euler,
        hence 3\,s steps for the 323\,s time constant of the block.
  \item \textbf{Probe}: the click sets the plotted point and redraws its curve from the stored results, no new solve; without
        a click the probe is the node nearest the centre of the bounding box.
\end{itemize}
